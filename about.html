THE FOUNDER

Meet the Vision Behind SYNORA.

SBE SHERDIL BIN EJAZ

My name is SBE Sherdil Bin Ejaz, and SYNORA represents a vision that started with something very simple: the desire to create a brand that people could connect with.

I believe a brand should be more than a name printed on a product. A real brand should carry an identity, a feeling and a reason to be remembered. That belief became the foundation of SYNORA.

From the beginning, the idea was to bring different worlds together. Men, Women and Kids should all have a place where they can discover accessories that match their personality and everyday life.

SYNORA is built around the idea that style does not have one age, one personality or one definition. A watch can represent confidence. A handbag can represent elegance. A bracelet can represent individuality. Even something as simple as a toy can create a memory.

My goal is to build SYNORA step by step into a name that people recognize, trust and feel proud to be connected with. Every product, every collection and every experience should reflect the same commitment: keep improving, keep learning and never stop dreaming bigger.

Sherdil Bin Ejaz
THE SYNORA STORY

Built With Purpose.

SYNORA is not designed to be just another accessories store. It is an evolving brand built around the belief that personal style can be expressed through the smallest details.

Our world is divided into three collections: Men, Women and Kids. Each collection has its own character while remaining connected by one recognizable SYNORA identity.

For Men, we focus on pieces that complement confidence and everyday style. For Women, we celebrate elegance, personality and individuality. For Kids, we create a world of fun, imagination and memorable little details.

The journey of SYNORA is about continuous growth. We want to listen, learn, improve and create an experience that becomes better with every step.

The ambition is not simply to sell more products. The ambition is to build a brand that people remember because of how it makes them feel.
